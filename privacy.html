Privacy Policy

Listing Studio · Effective 24 November 2026

Listing Studio (“Listing Studio”, “we”) is a private tracker that Etsy sellers and their teams use to keep track of which product listings still need new images. It is operated by Bilal Umer (“the operator”). This policy explains exactly what information the service handles, why, where it is kept and what your choices are. It is written to match how the service really works.

1. Information we handle

We do not collect your name, profile photo, phone number, location, payment information, Gmail, Google Drive files, contacts or calendar. We do not ask Google for those permissions and cannot see them. We never see or receive your Google password.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and to check, on every request, that you are still allowed to open the store you asked for.
  • To show, store and update the tracker content for the stores you are allowed to open.
  • To keep the service secure, for example to detect repeated or invalid sign-in attempts.

We do not use information for advertising, profiling, marketing or analytics. We do not sell, rent or trade information. We do not use it to train AI models.

3. Google user data

Listing Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: the only Google user data we receive is your email address; we use it solely to authenticate you and to check your access; we do not transfer it to anyone except as needed to run the service described here or when the law requires it; we do not use it for advertising; and no person reads it except for security, to comply with law, or to provide support you ask for.

4. Where information is stored and for how long

  • Store data and access records live in Google Sheets and in the private settings of a Google Apps Script project, both inside the operator’s Google account and protected by Google’s security. They are kept until the store administrator removes them or you ask for deletion.
  • Sign-in records are short-lived. A one-time sign-in record exists for at most 5 minutes and is deleted the moment it is used. A note that a sign-in link has been used is kept for up to 6 hours to stop it being used twice.
  • Your session is a signed token held in your browser’s memory and tab storage so you stay signed in for up to 12 hours. It is not a tracking cookie. A temporary random value used to bind sign-in to your browser is kept in your browser storage during sign-in and removed afterwards. “Sign out” ends your sessions on the server as well.
  • Fonts. The application page loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when the page opens, as for any web font.
  • Sign-in relay page. After Google sign-in you briefly pass through a static page on GitHub Pages that forwards the sign-in result to the application. It stores nothing and runs no analytics. GitHub, as the host of any web page, may keep ordinary server logs under its own policy.

5. Who we share information with

Only the service providers needed to run Listing Studio: Google (sign-in, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script hosting) and GitHub (hosting of these public pages and the sign-in relay). Within a store, people with access can see that store’s content. Administrators with permission to manage the team can see the email addresses of people on that store. Stores cannot see each other.

6. Security

Access is by invitation only and is checked on the server on every request. Sign-in uses Google’s OAuth with browser binding, single-use sign-in links and signed, expiring sessions. Disabling or removing someone blocks their next request immediately, even if they are signed in. Secrets such as signing keys are kept only on the server and are never published on these pages. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems promptly.

7. Your choices

  • Revoke Google access: you can review and remove Listing Studio’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Access, correction or deletion: ask your store’s administrator, or contact us below, and we will help you see, correct or delete the information that relates to you.
  • Sign out at any time from the “Access denied” or sign-in screen.

8. Children

Listing Studio is a business tool and is not directed to children. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way Listing Studio handles information changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
