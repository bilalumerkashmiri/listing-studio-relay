Signing you in…

Private tool · access by invitation

Know exactly which Etsy listings still need images.

Listing Studio is a simple, shared tracker for Etsy sellers and their design teams. Paste your listing links, and everyone sees what is done, what is left and what to watch out for.

Paste links, get a clean list

Add one or many Etsy listing links. The listing number is found automatically and duplicates are flagged straight away.

One switch per listing

Designers mark “Images Done” with a single switch. Completion time is recorded in Pakistan time (Asia/Karachi).

Notes where they matter

Administrators leave special instructions on any listing. Team members read them before they start.

One workspace per store

Every Etsy store has its own tracker and its own people. Stores never see each other’s data.

Who can use it

Listing Studio is not a public service. Each store’s administrator chooses who may open that store, either as an Administrator (adds and edits listings) or a Team Member (reads notes and marks listings done). If you were invited, open the store link your administrator sent you.

Signing in

You sign in with your Google account. Listing Studio receives only your email address from Google, to check that you have been invited. It never receives your password and never reads your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar. Read the Privacy Policy for the full details.

Not invited yet? Ask the person who runs your Etsy store’s tracker to add the Google account you want to use.
